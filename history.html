Topic 1: Historical Develepment of HTML

Early Development

HTML was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1991 with 18 basic tags that focused heavily on simple text and hyperlinks within text. Early developers initially used complex layout tables to organize layouts, which proved resource-heavy and hard for modern screen processors to navigate cleanly.

Recent Developments

Modern layouts utilize dedicated processing infrastructures to read and handle site structural markups seamlessly across unique operational applications:

Browser Engine Primary Corperations Target Web Environments
Blink Engine Google Powers Chrom, Microsft Edge, and Opera platforms.
Webkit Engine Apple Powers Safari for iOS, iPadOS and mac OS.
Gecko Engine Mozilla Independent, open-source engine that powers Firefox.
